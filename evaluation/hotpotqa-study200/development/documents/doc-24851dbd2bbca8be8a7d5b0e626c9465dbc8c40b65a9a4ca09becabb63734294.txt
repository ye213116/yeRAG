List of Oasis band members

Oasis were an English rock band from Manchester. Formed in 1991, the group originally featured Gallagher brothers Liam (lead vocals) and Noel (guitar, vocals), as well as guitarist and keyboardist Paul "Bonehead" Arthurs, bassist Paul "Guigsy" McGuigan and drummer Tony McCarroll. After signing to Creation Records in 1993, the band released their debut album "Definitely Maybe" in 1994, which topped the UK Albums Chart and went on to sell over 15 million copies worldwide. In April 1995, after the recording and release of the single "Some Might Say", McCarroll was fired from Oasis. He was replaced by Alan White, who performed on the band's second album "(What's the Story) Morning Glory?", released in 1995. McGuigan briefly left the band during a tour in September 1995 and was temporarily replaced by Scott McLeod, although he returned a few weeks later. The band's third album "Be Here Now" was released in 1997, following the previous two releases by topping the UK Albums Chart.
