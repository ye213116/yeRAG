Definitely Maybe Tour

Definitely Maybe Tour was a world concert tour by English band Oasis in support of their hugely successful debut album "Definitely Maybe". The tour, which spanned the UK, Europe, Japan, the US and Canada, included 143 shows over a period of several months in 1994 and 1995 amidst 10 different tour legs. The tour started on 6 February 1994 with a short concert at Gleneagles, Scotland, and ended on 22 April 1995 at the Sheffield Arena, which featured an acoustic debut of the future hit Don't Look Back in Anger and was also the last concert to feature original drummer Tony McCarroll.
