Standish Vereker, 7th Viscount Gort

Standish Robert Gage Prendergast Vereker, 7th Viscount Gort {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} (12 February 1888 – 21 May 1975) was an Anglo-Irish peer, connoisseur and collector of fine art, antiques, and objets d'art, whose seat was at Hamsterley Hall, County Durham. He was appointed High Sheriff of Durham in 1934.
