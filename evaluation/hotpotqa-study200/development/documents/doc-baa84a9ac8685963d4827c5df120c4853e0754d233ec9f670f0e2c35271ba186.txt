No. 12 Elementary Flying Training School RAAF

No. 12 Elementary Flying Training School was a Royal Australian Air Force (RAAF) pilot training unit of World War II. It was established in October 1941 at Bundaberg Airport and provided introductory flying training to new RAAF pilots. No. 12 Elementary Flying Training School relocated to Lowood Airfield on 12 January 1942. Due to arrival of the United States Army Air Corp at Lowood in March, the aircraft, staff and trainees were allocated to other units, with the unit being disbanded in April.
