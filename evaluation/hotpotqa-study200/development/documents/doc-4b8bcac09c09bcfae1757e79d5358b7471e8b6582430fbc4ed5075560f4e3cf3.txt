Cleo Ice Queen

Clementina Mulenga (born June 14, 1989), professionally known as Cleo or Cleo Ice Queen is a Zambian born hip hop recording artist, Proflight Zambia brand ambassador, Maximum Diva Woman Condom brand ambassador, model and Television presenter. She is best known for her role in Big Brother Africa (The chase) in 2013 and 2015 AFRIMMA Best Female in Southern Africa.
