Dr. Dolittle: Million Dollar Mutts

Dr. Dolittle: Million Dollar Mutts (also known as Dr. Dolittle 5 or Dr. Dolittle 5: Million Dollar Mutts) is a 2009 American comedy film, starring Kyla Pratt and Norm Macdonald. It was released on May 19, 2009 and like its predecessor, "" (2008), was a direct-to-DVD release.
