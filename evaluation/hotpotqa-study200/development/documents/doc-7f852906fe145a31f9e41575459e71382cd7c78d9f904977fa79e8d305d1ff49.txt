Idrees Ul Haq

Idrees Ul Haq, full name Mirza Mohammad Idrees ul Haq Beigh, is a Kashmiri innovator and activist He is best known for innovations , social work and was honoured in 2005 by the Governor of Jammu and Kashmir in recognition of his social work; however, he renounced his award in July 201 in protest at the Ramban firing incident. He was the first youth from Jammu and Kashmir to be invited for the Technology Transfer program at Israel
