Augustus Nicodemus Lopes

"The Rev. Dr." Augustus Nicodemus Gomes Lopes, born in Paraíba, Brazil, is a Presbyterian minister, Calvinist theologian, writer and professor. He was Chancellor of Mackenzie Presbyterian University from 2003 to 2013. He is considered one of the greatest Brazilian conservative theologians. He is married to Hendrika Hermina Schalkwijk Lopes and has four children.
