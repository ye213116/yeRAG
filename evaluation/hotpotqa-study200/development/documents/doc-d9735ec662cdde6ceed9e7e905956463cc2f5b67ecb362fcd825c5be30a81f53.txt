Miles Doleac

Miles Christopher Doleac is an American film and television actor, director, writer and producer. Along with his film career, he is also a singer, songwriter, theater actor, author, and professor. He has had acting roles in several films and television shows since 2011 including "Treme, Sleepy Hollow, American Horror Story, Salem, Complications, Roots," and several episodes of the CW's "Containment". He also has acting roles in the recent films "The Magnificent Seven" and "Don't Kill It."
