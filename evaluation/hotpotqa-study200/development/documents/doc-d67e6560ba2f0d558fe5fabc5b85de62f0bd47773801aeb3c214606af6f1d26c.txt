Disco Ruined My Life

Disco Ruined My Life, also known as DRML, is a fashion label created by Maurice Uzzan, award winning designer from Graniph Designs  based in Rome, Italy. DRML is a fashion label that showcases "high fashion" icons such as Comme des Garçons, Hermès, Givenchy, Yves Saint Laurent and Alexander McQueen with ironic symbols. Many of the designs consist of the original logo, however, changed to give a different meaning while still recognizable.
