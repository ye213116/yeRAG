Mossad

Mossad (Hebrew: הַמוֹסָד‎ , ] ; Arabic: الموساد‎ ‎ , "al-Mōsād ", ] ; literally meaning "the Institute"), short for "HaMossad leModiʿin uleTafkidim Meyuḥadim " (Hebrew: המוסד למודיעין ולתפקידים מיוחדים‎ , meaning "Institute for Intelligence and Special Operations"), is the national intelligence agency of Israel. It is one of the main entities in the Israeli Intelligence Community, along with Aman (military intelligence) and Shin Bet (internal security).
