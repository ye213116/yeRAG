Gurdeep Kohli

Gurdeep Kohli is an Indian actress, known for her popular roles as Dr. Juhi Singh in Star TV's "Sanjivani", Vedika on Zee TV's "Sindoor Tere Naam Ka", Himani Singh on Disney Channel's "Best of Luck Nikki" and Sethji Ahilya Devi in Zee TV 's "Sethji".She replaced Prachi Desai as Bani in Kasamh Se in which she played female lead.
