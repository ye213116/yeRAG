Pier Luigi de Borgia, 1st duke of Gandía

Pier Luigi de Borgia, 1st duke of Gandía (Spanish: "Pedro Luis de Borja" , Latin: "Petrus Ludovicus de Boria" ) (1458 or 1460–1488 or 1491) was a Valencian noble. Pier Luigi was the son of Cardinal Rodrigo Borgia (later Pope Alexander VI), and half-brother of Cesare Borgia, Gioffre Borgia, Giovanni Borgia and Lucrezia Borgia.
