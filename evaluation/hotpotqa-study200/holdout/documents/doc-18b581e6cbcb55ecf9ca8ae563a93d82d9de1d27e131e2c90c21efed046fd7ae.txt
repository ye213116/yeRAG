Lost in the Former West

Lost in the Former West was the final album released by The Fatima Mansions, continuing the focus on hard-rock anthems that had begun on "Valhalla Avenue". As with "Viva Dead Ponies", the track listing of "Lost in the Former West" as released in the US differed from the UK version, incorporating "Something Bad" and "Go Home Bible Mike" from "Valhalla Avenue", while excluding "Sunken Cities".
