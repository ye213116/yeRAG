Murad Machaev

Murad Magomedkhanovich Machaev (Russian: Мурад Магомедханович Мачаев ; born December 3, 1986 in Dagestan) is a Russian mixed martial artist of Avar heritage who currently fighting in the lightweight division for the Eurasia Fight Nights Global (EFN), Bellator MMA veteran, he is the winner of the 2011 Fedor Emelianenko Cup.
