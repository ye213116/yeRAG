Sophie, Duchess of Hohenberg

Sophie, Duchess von Hohenberg (Czech: "Žofie Marie Josefína Albína hraběnka Chotková z Chotkova a Vojnína" ; German: "Sophie Maria Josephine Albina Gräfin Chotek von Chotkow und Wognin" ; 1 March 1868 – 28 June 1914), was the morganatic wife of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria, the heir to the Austro-Hungarian throne. Their assassination in Sarajevo sparked a series of events that eventually led to World War I.
