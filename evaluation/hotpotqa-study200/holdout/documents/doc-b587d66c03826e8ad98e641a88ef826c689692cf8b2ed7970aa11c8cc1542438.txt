Cemindo Tower

Gama Tower is a skyscraper at Jalan H R Rasuna Said, South Jakarta, Indonesia. It was named as "Rasuna Tower" and "Cemindo Tower" during construction period, but finally named as "Gama Tower". It is a 289-meter roof tall & 310-meter tip tall skyscraper with 69 floors above and 4 floors below the ground. Land area of the tower development area is 1.6 hectares. It has a parking lot of over 1100 vehicles. At present the tower is the tallest building in Indonesia and 74th tallest building in the world. The tower is a mixed office and hotel building. Construction of the tower was started in 2011, topped off in 2015 and opened in August, 2016.
