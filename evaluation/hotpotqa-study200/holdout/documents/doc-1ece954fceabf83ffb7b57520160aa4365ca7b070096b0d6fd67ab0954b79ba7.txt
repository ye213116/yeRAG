Isabel Bloom

Isabel Bloom (February 20, 1908 – May 1, 2001) was an artist who was born Isabel Scherer in Galveston, Texas, and raised in Davenport, Iowa. She learned sculpture while studying at the Stone City Art Colony in central Iowa under Florence Sprague. In the two summers she spent there, she met fellow artist and future husband John Bloom, and the two married in 1938.
