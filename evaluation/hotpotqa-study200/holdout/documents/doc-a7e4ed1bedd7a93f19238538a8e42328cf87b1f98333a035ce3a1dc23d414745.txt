Alexander Emelianenko

Alexander Vladimirovich Emelianenko (Russian: Александр Владимирович Емельяненко , "Aleksandr Vladimirovich Emel'janenko" ] ; born (1981--) 02, 1981 ) is a Russian mixed martial artist. He is a three-time Russian national Combat Sambo champion and three-time world Combat Sambo champion in the absolute division. Emelianenko was convicted of aggravated sexual assault in May 2015 and was released early on parole on October 26, 2016. He is the younger brother of Fedor Emelianenko.
