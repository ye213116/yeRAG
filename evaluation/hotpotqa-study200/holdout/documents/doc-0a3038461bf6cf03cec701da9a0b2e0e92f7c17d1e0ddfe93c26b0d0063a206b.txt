Newcastle Airport (New South Wales)

The Newcastle Airport (IATA: NTL, ICAO: YWLM) is 8 NM north of Newcastle, New South Wales (27 km by road) in Port Stephens. It is the 12th busiest airport in Australia, handling over 1.25 million passengers in the year ended 30 June 2017, an increase of 6.6% on the previous year. The airport occupies a 28 ha site on the southern border of RAAF Base Williamtown.
