Aliya Mustafina

Aliya Fargatovna Mustafina (Russian: Алия Фархатовна Мустафина ] ; Tatar: Алия Фәрһәт кызы Мостафина , "Aliä Färhät qızı Mostafina " ; born 30 September 1994) is an artistic gymnast from Russia. She is the 2010 world champion in the all-around, the 2012 and 2016 Olympic uneven bars champion, and a seven-time Olympic medalist.
