Connor Van Vuuren

Connor Van Vuuren (/'vjʊərən/) is a stuntman, actor and director from Sydney, Australia. Van Vuuren, alongside his brother Christiaan Van Vuuren, created the TV mini-series "Bondi Hipsters". He has directed and starred in ABC2's "Soul Mates" series in 2014 as "Phoenix" with his brother Christiaan Van Vuuren and Nicholas Boshier, and as a stunt actor in "" (2015), "" (2014), and "The Wolverine" (2013).
