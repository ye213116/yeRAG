Tandav (2014 film)

Tandav is the second movie produced by Mohaan Dotel and also second movie by director Murray Kerr. The movie marks the debut of the rap singer Aashish Rana (Laure) in Nepali film industry and features Namrata Shrestha, Anup Baral, Alan Gurung, Beepin Karki in lead role. The film was released on August 22, 2014.
