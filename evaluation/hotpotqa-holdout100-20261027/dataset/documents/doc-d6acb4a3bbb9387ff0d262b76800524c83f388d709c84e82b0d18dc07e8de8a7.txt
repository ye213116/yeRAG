Nearly Lost You

"Nearly Lost You" is a song by the American alternative rock group Screaming Trees. It is the first single released in support of their sixth album "Sweet Oblivion". Perhaps their best-known song, it was a moderate success on modern rock radio, partly because of its appearance on the to the 1992 Cameron Crowe film "Singles". In addition to the soundtrack, "Nearly Lost You" also appeared on the band's 1992 breakthrough album "Sweet Oblivion". It also appears on the soundtrack to the 2007 baseball video game The Bigs, and is available as downloadable content for the Rock Band series, and is in the main soundtrack of Guitar Hero 5. The song was covered in 2013 by Rogue Wave.
