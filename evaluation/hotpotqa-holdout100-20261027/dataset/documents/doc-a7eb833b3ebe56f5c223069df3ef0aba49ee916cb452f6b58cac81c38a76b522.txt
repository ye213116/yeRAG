Sutan Amrull

Sutan Amrull ( ; born June 14, 1974), also known as Raja and Raja Gemini, is an American make-up artist and drag performer. He is best known for his work on the reality television show "America's Next Top Model", serving as the show's make-up artist for nine cycles (fourth through twelve), and as the winner of Season 3 of "RuPaul's Drag Race". Amrull's clients include Dita von Teese, Pamela Anderson, Paulina Porizkova, Iman, Tyra Banks, Iggy Azalea, RuPaul, and Twiggy. Since 2009, Amrull has been make-up artist to singer Adam Lambert for print media, live U.S. appearances, and Lambert's international 2010 Glam Nation Tour.
