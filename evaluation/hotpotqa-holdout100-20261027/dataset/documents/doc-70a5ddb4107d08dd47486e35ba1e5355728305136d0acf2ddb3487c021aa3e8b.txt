Mahakavi Kalidasu

Mahakavi Kalidasu (Telugu: మహాకవి కాళిదాసు) is a 1960 Telugu, movie produced by K. Nagamani, P. Suri Babu on Sarani Productions banner and directed by Kamalakara Kameswara Rao. Starring Akkineni Nageswara Rao played the title role Sriranjani as the female lead and music composed by Pendyala Nageswara Rao.The movie is based on the 1955 Kannada movie "Mahakavi Kalidasa" which also went on to be remade in Tamil in 1966 as "Mahakavi Kalidas".
