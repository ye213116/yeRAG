Ian Browne (musician)

Ian Browne (born November 12, 1973 ) is a Canadian-born musician and composer, known currently as being a member of Vancouver-based rock and roll band The Prettys. He was formerly with psych-blues band No Sinner, and before that notably as a member of multi-platinum-selling 90's Canadian rock group the Matthew Good Band. Browne plays drums and performs backup vocals on The Prettys' upcoming second LP "Soirée". He also played on No Sinner's debut EP "Boo Hoo Hoo", and played drums and produced tracks on their 2016 LP "Old Habits Die Hard". He can also be heard on the Matthew Good Band albums "Last of the Ghetto Astronauts", "Raygun", "Underdogs", "Lo-Fi B-Sides", "Beautiful Midnight", "Loser Anthems", "Audio of Being", and the greatest hits compilation "In a Coma".
