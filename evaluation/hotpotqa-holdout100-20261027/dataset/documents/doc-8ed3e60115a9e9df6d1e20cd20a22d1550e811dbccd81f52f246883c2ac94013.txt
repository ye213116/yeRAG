Plaga Zombie: Zona Mutante

Plaga Zombie:Zona Mutante is a 2001 Argentine horror film directed and written by Pablo Parés and Hernán Sáez with Berta Muñiz and Gabriel Grieco who all starred in the film. It is the second entry in the "Plaga Zombie film series" as the sequel to the 1997 film "Plaga Zombie" and followed by the 2012 film "".
