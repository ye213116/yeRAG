Nobody Does It Better (Nate Dogg song)

"Nobody Does It Better" is a song by American hip hop and R&B recording artist Nate Dogg, featuring vocals from American Hip hop recording artist Warren G. It was released in June 1998 as the second single released from the studio album "G-Funk Classics, Vols. 1 & 2" (1998). The song produced by Warren G. The song samples and contains an interpolation from “Let's Get Closer” by Atlantic Starr.
