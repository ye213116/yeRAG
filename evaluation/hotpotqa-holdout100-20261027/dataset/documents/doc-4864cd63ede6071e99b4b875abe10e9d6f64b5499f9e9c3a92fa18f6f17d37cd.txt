Treat Huey

Treat Conrad Huey ( ) (born 28 August 1985) is a Filipino-American tennis player who represents the Philippines in international competitions. He turned professional in 2008 and he started representing the Philippines in the Davis Cup and the Southeast Asian Games in 2009. Huey specializes in doubles and has reached eighteen finals on the ATP World Tour. He won titles at the 2012 Citi Open, 2013 Swiss Indoors, and 2014 Aegon International alongside Dominic Inglot, 2015 Estoril Open with Scott Lipsky, and 2015 St. Petersburg Open and 2015 Malaysian Open, Kuala Lumpur with Henri Kontinen.
