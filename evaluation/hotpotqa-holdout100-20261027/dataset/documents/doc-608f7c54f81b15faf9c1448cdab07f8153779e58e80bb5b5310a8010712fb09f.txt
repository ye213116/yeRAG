Voice of Silence (2013 film)

Voice of Silence (Persian: حق سکوت‎ ‎ ) is a 2013 Iranian film written and directed by Mohammad Hadi Naeiji. The movie has won the Crystal Simorgh for Best Screenplay at the 32nd Fajr International Film Festival. It was produced by the award-winning writer and director Reza Mirkarimi the director of A Cube of Sugar.
