Ms Panda and Mr Hedgehog

Panda and Hedgehog () is a 2012 South Korean romantic comedy television series starring Lee Donghae of Super Junior and Yoon Seung-ah. Produced by Song Hae-sung's TV production venture Lion Fish, it aired on Channel A from August 18 to October 7, 2012 on Saturdays and Sundays at 21:55 for 16 episodes.
