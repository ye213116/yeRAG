Tudor Barn, Eltham

The Tudor Barn is a large brick barn in Eltham in the Royal Borough of Greenwich. It was built in 1525 by William Roper. The Ropers lived next door in a manor house in the center of a moat for several years. William married Margaret More, the daughter of Thomas More, who at the time was the lord chancellor to Henry VIII. It is a Grade II* listed building (as Well Hall Art Gallery).
