Big Bambu

Big Bambú is the second album by Cheech & Chong, released in 1972. The name "Big Bambu" is a reference to a type of rolling paper made by the Bambu company; the original LP sleeve was designed by Ernie Cefalu and manufactured to look like a giant rolling paper package, and originally contained a giant rolling paper with the record. Vinyl copies with the rolling paper have become hard to find. The original CD packaging has been slightly reformatted, and does not contain rolling papers.
