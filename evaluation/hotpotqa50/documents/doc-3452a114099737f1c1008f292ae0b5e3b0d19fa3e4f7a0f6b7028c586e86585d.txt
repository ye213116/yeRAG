Hekmat

Hekmat (Persian: حكمت‎ ‎  –"Wisdom") was the first Persian-language newspaper to be published in Egypt, as well as the first Persian journal to be published in an Arab country. Founded and managed by the Iranian expat Mohammad-Mahdi Tabrizi (died 1914), a physician by profession, it was published from 20 September 1892 until 30 May 1911. "Hekmat" carried mostly news, but also feature a variety of articles on political and social issues. Despite being published in an "Arab country", it avoided the usage of Arabic terms and compoun words as well as Arabicized forms of non-Arab words throughouts its texts.
