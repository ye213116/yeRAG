The Race (Wiz Khalifa song)

"The Race" is a song by American rapper Wiz Khalifa from his third studio album "Rolling Papers". The song was written by Khalifa and produced by Eric Dan and Jeremy "Big Jerm" Kulousek for ID Labs. "The Race" was released as the third single from "Rolling Papers" on March 8, 2011, and debuted and peaked at number 66 on the "Billboard" Hot 100.
