Amka

Amka (Hebrew: עַמְקָה‎ ), also known in Arabic as Amqa (Arabic: عمقا‎ ‎ ), is a moshav in the Matte Asher Regional Council of Israel's Northern District, near Acre. The location of the moshav roughly corresponds the former Arab village, depopulated during the 1948 Arab–Israeli War. Yemenite Jews, who arrived from the southern Arab country of Yemen, founded the village's successor Amka in 1949. In 2016 its population was 706 .
